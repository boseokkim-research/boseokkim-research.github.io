About Boseok Kim / 김보석

A scholarly path
across contexts.

I am an educational psychology researcher. I conducted my doctoral research at the University of Cambridge.

My work examines culture, relationships and educational contexts in young people’s lives. I am particularly interested in how children and adolescents experience gratitude, autonomy and wellbeing, and how educational interventions can respond to differences between learners.

I trained in psychology and education at Aberystwyth University, completed an MA specialising in educational psychology at Seoul National University, and pursued doctoral research at Cambridge. My doctoral thesis has been approved.
